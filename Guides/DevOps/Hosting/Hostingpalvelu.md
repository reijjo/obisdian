# Hostingpalvelu
<https://www.hostingpalvelu.fi/>

## Adding Cloudflare nameservers
1. Click *Continue to activation* after the nameservers list
2. Cloudflare gives two nameservers that needs to be added to the hostingpalvelu settings
3. In hostingpalvelut website go to _Domainisi_ --> _Nimipalvelimet_ and add those nameservers there
4. Click *Muuta nimipalvelimia* and wait for those to update
5. Then back to _Cloudflare_ and click *I updated my nameservers* button
6. And then we wait for the nameservers to register. You can click the *Check nameservers now* button once in a while to see if those are updated or just reload the page. When the domain status changes to _Active_ we continue to the next step
